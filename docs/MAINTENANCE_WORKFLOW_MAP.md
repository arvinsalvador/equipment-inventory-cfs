# Maintenance Workflow Map

## Scope

This map documents the stabilized maintenance lifecycle covering AI recommendations, maintenance schedules, maintenance requests, work orders, evidence, audit activity, and notifications.

## AI Recommendation Lifecycle

1. A recommendation is generated by `MaintenanceRecommendationEngine` with status `Open` and action status `Pending`.
2. Authorized administrators may review, resolve, dismiss, approve, reject, cancel, or execute the action.
3. Existing architecture intentionally executes approved recommendation actions directly into one of these targets:
   - Maintenance schedule for preventive, due-soon, initial, or warranty recommendations.
   - Work order for corrective or inspection recommendations.
   - Linked evidence guidance for evidence-related recommendations.
   - Monitor-only action when no operational record is needed.
4. Duplicate execution is blocked when the recommendation already has a linked schedule, linked work order, or executed action.
5. Linked work order completion, verification, or beyond-repair closure resolves the recommendation.
6. Linked work order reopening moves the recommendation back to `Reviewed` while keeping the created work order link.
7. Linked work order cancellation marks the recommendation action `Cancelled` and moves the recommendation to `Reviewed` for follow-up.
8. Linked maintenance schedule completion resolves the recommendation.
9. Linked maintenance schedule cancellation marks the recommendation action `Cancelled` and moves the recommendation to `Reviewed` for follow-up.

## Maintenance Schedule Lifecycle

1. Schedules start as `Upcoming`, `Due soon`, `Due today`, `Overdue`, or `In progress` depending on stored status and scheduled date display logic.
2. Due and overdue display state is calculated from `scheduled_date` for incomplete schedules.
3. Completion requires an incomplete, non-cancelled schedule.
4. Completing a schedule records completion metadata, updates equipment maintenance dates, and creates the next recurring schedule when frequency supports recurrence.
5. Cancelling requires a reason and stores cancellation metadata.
6. When a schedule came from a recommendation, completion or cancellation synchronizes the linked recommendation outcome.

## Maintenance Request Lifecycle

1. Requests are submitted against equipment by an authorized user.
2. Submitted or for-review requests can be approved or rejected.
3. Approved requests can be converted to one work order.
4. Duplicate conversion is blocked by the unique `maintenance_request_id` work-order relationship and by model-level conversion checks.
5. Request approval does not create a work order automatically; conversion is a separate action.
6. Converted, rejected, and cancelled requests are treated as closed.

## Work Order Lifecycle

1. Work orders may be created from approved maintenance requests or directly from approved AI recommendation actions when the existing recommendation architecture calls for it.
2. Open workflow states include `Available`, `Assigned`, `Accepted`, `In progress`, `On hold`, `Awaiting parts`, `For verification`, and `Reopened`.
3. Terminal states include `Completed`, `Beyond repair`, `Rejected`, and `Cancelled`.
4. Completion requires action performed, final equipment condition, final operational status, completion remarks, and at least one after-maintenance evidence record.
5. Beyond-repair closure requires findings, beyond-repair reason, recommended action, and at least two beyond-repair evidence records.
6. Verification is allowed only for for-verification, completed, or beyond-repair work and blocks users from verifying their own accepted work.
7. Completion and beyond-repair closure update the equipment final condition and operational status.
8. Linked recommendation outcomes are synchronized when work orders complete, verify, reopen, or cancel.

## Evidence Enforcement

1. After-maintenance evidence is required before normal completion or verification from `For verification`.
2. Beyond-repair evidence requires at least two evidence records.
3. Recommendation evidence actions do not create fake uploads; they link the relevant work order and keep the action approved until real evidence is supplied through the work-order evidence workflow.

## Audit And Notifications

1. Model lifecycle events and resource actions write audit entries for creates, updates, status changes, approvals, conversions, completions, and recommendation actions.
2. System notification services remain separate from workflow transitions and can detect open work orders, overdue schedules, and unresolved recommendations without changing business state.

## Stabilization Notes

- No schema change was required for this stabilization.
- The existing direct recommendation-to-work-order and recommendation-to-schedule architecture was preserved.
- The primary gap fixed was downstream lifecycle completion not updating linked recommendation resolution state.
